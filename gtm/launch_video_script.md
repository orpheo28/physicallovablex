## Two formats
**16:9 — 1920×1080 (X, LinkedIn, YouTube, landing page)**
- Upscale the 1440×900 takes to fit 1728×1080 and centre them on a paper `#F7F6F3` frame (thin side margins), or crop to 16:9 by trimming the top bar (which also removes the lockup, see below).
- Text: Inter Tight 600 for lines, Archivo condensed uppercase for chapter titles, Geist Mono for numbers and footnotes; title safe area 5 % from each edge.

**9:16 — 1080×1920 (Reels, TikTok, Shorts, LinkedIn mobile)**
- Do not squeeze the desktop UI. Use **crops** of the 1440×900 takes, each ≈ 1080 px wide: the 3D viewer, the cost column, the diff card, the code panel, the stepper. Pan inside a crop rather than cutting more often.
- Layout: chapter title in the top third (below the top 250 px reserved for platform UI), footage in the middle, the one-line claim above the bottom 350 px (captions, buttons). Footnotes in Geist Mono at ≥ 28 px so they stay legible on a phone.
- Same beat map and timings as the master; export a separate 9:16 timeline, not an auto-reframe.

**Both:** H.264, 30 fps, −14 LUFS; burned-in text (no reliance on platform captions); first frame = shot 1 so the thumbnail reads "Describe it. See it. Refine it. Make it."
