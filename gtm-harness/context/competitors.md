- **Text-to-CAD tools:** RapidDirect AI Creator Lab ([GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/15/3311907/0/en/Breaking-Design-Barriers-RapidDirect-s-AI-Creator-Lab-Allows-Anyone-to-Speak-Their-Product-Into-Existence.html)), Adam, Zoo.

These players deliver **parts for engineers** or **AI-generated design**, not a finished consumer product with a guarantee.

**So what:** prompt-to-product is no longer an empty space, and nobody publishes proof of delivery. Carrying the risk is the gap to take (`02_RECHERCHE/parallel/B_concurrence.md`, "Lecture").
