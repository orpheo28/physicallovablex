# Playbook: lighthouse ambassadors and the one case

**Trigger:** a lighthouse founder's real product reaches a real factory quote from its Factory Pack (the North Star event, `context/metrics.md`), and at W5-W6 when the case is written (deck slide 9). Everything below is Hypothesis until the first quote.

**Source:** Maja Voje, ["Can you get to your first million with a single case study?"](https://knowledge.gtmstrategist.com/p/can-you-get-to-your-first-million) (`04_LIVRABLE/GTM_Lessons_Substack_MajaVoje.md`): a company is a machine that replicates one sales case; document one complete journey and make it the main sales tool; 1 ICP + 1 offer + 1 channel. `PRD.md` §17: "one complete case (prototype → Factory Pack → factory quote) becomes the main sales artifact in W5-W6 before widening". Communities: Voje's 5-useful-comments rule ("Unleash the Power of Communities") and Verna's warning that most communities become support forums for negativity (`02_RECHERCHE/lovable.md`, Vidéo 1).

## Part 1: document ONE complete case
**Pick the case** (one only, the most complete):
1. A real prototype, a human-signed Factory Pack, a real factory quote returned from the pack (target ≤ 5 working days, Hypothesis).
2. Ideally a sample or an order placed; if not by W6, publish up to the quote and extend later.
3. The founder agrees **in writing** to be named, or to an anonymised version ("consumer-electronics founder, wearable").

**Write it** as `outputs/YYYY-MM-DD-case-<product-or-anonymised>.md`, one page:
```
Before: public signal (date, quote), coded cause (call-debrief tag), what they had tried
Pack: the 3 findings that mattered, each with its trust label; engineer sign-off date
Factory: how it was reached (intermediary type), RFQ date, quote date, clarification rounds
Outcome: sample / order / not yet; landed price vs pledge price only if the founder allows it
Founder's words: ≤ 2 short quotes, approved by the founder
What we would do differently
```
No BOM, file or price term in the repo unless the founder approves that exact line (`README.md`, privacy).

**Use it everywhere, before widening:** the follow-up after a PVP yes (`skills/pvp-pack`), calls (`playbooks/founder-replied.md` step 5), intermediaries (`playbooks/factory-intro.md` step 2), the deck, and the llms.txt case link post-go (`skills/aeo-check`).

## Part 2: turn the 10 lighthouse founders into ambassadors
| Step | Give first | Then ask |
|---|---|---|
| 1. After their pack | Walk-through in 30 min (deck slide 9, W3); fix what they flag | Permission to count their outcome in the published rates |
| 2. After their quote | Early access to new Studio features; their pack re-run on each release | One intro to another founder near production |
| 3. Seed the group | A small private founder channel, and the in-person atelier (deck slide 9, W2) | Share one production lesson each; no promotion |
| 4. Post-go | Credit on the case page and in posts (`playbooks/build-in-public.md`) | Co-host one "build a product" weekend (`playbooks/free-as-marketing.md`) |

## Rules
- Support questions go to a direct line, not the group, so the group stays about production lessons.
- No paid ambassadors, no testimonial before a real outcome, no public name without written consent.
- Measure: intros given per founder, second design or pack handed to a factory (founder PMF signal, `context/metrics.md`), case views that lead to a call.
- Never present fictional demo data as part of the case (`context/profile.md`).
