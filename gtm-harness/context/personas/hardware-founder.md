# Persona: the hardware founder (user and buyer)

This is a founder or CEO of a small team who has funded a first or second hardware product through crowdfunding and manufactures it in Asia. In segment A, the public contact is the founder (`outputs/accounts.csv`, `contact` column).

**Observed** facts have a source. **Hypothesis** items have none yet and are the things campaign 01 must test.

## Their days (observed)
- **Writing public updates to backers about production.**
  - Pilet update #20 announced the redesign ([c33tech](https://c33tech.com/blog/2026/04/how_not_to_pilet_a_kickstarter/)).
  - Circular: "By Week 5, we were down to 60 remaining orders" ([Circular](https://www.circular.xyz/post/circular-ring-2-release-notes-oct-2025)).
- **Answering angry comments one by one.** The GAMR creator explains the raw-material delays in the Kickstarter comments ([Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/active-gaming-pad/gamr-active-gaming-play-pad/comments?comment=Q29tbWVudC00ODAyODk5Ng%3D%3D), medium confidence).
- **Running fulfilment tools** such as PledgeBox and BackerKit (`outputs/accounts.csv`, AERIONN and Peak Design rows).

## What blocks them (observed)
- **Component quality.** At AIVELA, "About half of the units made in that batch passed final checks" ([Gadgets & Wearables](https://gadgetsandwearables.com/2026/02/12/aivela-ring-pro-shipping/)).
- **Certification.** HOVERAir AQUA's US backers were refunded after an FCC ban ([DroneXL](https://dronexl.co/2026/05/28/hoverair-aqua-global-launch-us-fcc-ban/)).
- **Speaking the factory's language.** Halliday's COO: "we didn't speak [the manufacturer's] language", with "many mistakes" on the G1 ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2216393/halliday-g2-smart-glasses/)).

## Who influences them
- **Backers (observed).**
  - Pilet backers built a refund site ([c33tech](https://c33tech.com/blog/2026/04/how_not_to_pilet_a_kickstarter/)).
  - The Minimal Phone drew BBB complaints ([iTechGuides](https://www.itechguides.com/with-backers-losing-patience-the-long-delayed-minimal-phone-was-rumored-to-ship-in-two-weeks-what-happened/)).
  - Smokpub is rated 1.7/5 on Trustpilot ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/smokpub.com)).
- **An engineering co-founder (observed).** Looktech has a CTO, Leo Tong. Blisstil's founder is also its engineer. The Minimal Company, Astrohaus and Soulscircuit each have two co-founders (`outputs/accounts.csv`).
- **Hypothesis:** the engineering co-founder owns the technical choices, and so holds a veto over any outside DFM or component recommendation.

## How they decide (hypothesis, to test)
- They decide alone or as a pair of co-founders, with no committee (`icp.md`).
- They buy when a promised date is already public and at risk, not before the campaign.
- They compare against the cost of a sourcing agent at 5 to 10% ([Dragon Sourcing](https://www.dragonsourcing.com/top-10-cost-insights-for-hiring-a-china-sourcing-agent/)) rather than against a SaaS subscription.
- The next product is the buying moment. Halliday is preparing the G2 after the G1's mistakes ([Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2216393/halliday-g2-smart-glasses/)).

## Implications for messaging
- Cite *their* signal: the update number and the date.
- Ask about the cause, never pitch.
- Talk to the founder and the engineering co-founder together.
