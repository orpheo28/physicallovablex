# Playbook: free as the marketing budget

**Trigger:** Phase 2 (land grab) starts: Phase 1 exit met (≥ 3 production runs started, `PRD.md` §18) **and** Hexa's go to go public. Not before: amplifying before real factories and delivery data would amplify a promise (deck slide 5, "where the analogy breaks"). Everything below is Hypothesis until the first event.

**Source:** Elena Verna, Lovable (`02_RECHERCHE/lovable.md`, Vidéo 1): "your free giveaways, whether it's part premium, whether it's discount codes or whatnot, has to be bigger than your paid marketing spend"; freemium is where Lovable's costs sit, "because we view freemium as actually marketing"; referrals measured with an internal score, "an earned channel that you cannot buy". She Builds (free Lovable for a day): 100,000+ new users, 120 events, 40 countries (same file, Vidéo 3). Tier 1 is free by design (`PRD.md` §17: "freemium = marketing budget").

## Rule 1: free budget > paid budget
- Paid acquisition is 0 while the conversion window is > 3 months or unknown (`context/metrics.md`). So the free budget wins by default; keep it that way when paid starts.
- **Count the LLM cost of free usage as marketing**, not as cost of goods: ≈ $0.34 per Make it and ≈ $0.01 per refine in one measured run (`PRODUIT.md`). Report it monthly next to any paid line.

## Rule 2: give it where trust already sits
Borrowed from Applied Intuition (distribution through the players who already hold trust, `02_RECHERCHE/lecons_inputs.md`). Four places, one at a time:
| Where | What we give | What we ask |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware hackathons | Free Studio + credits for every team; a human-reviewed pack for the winning team | Teams tag their project; organiser shares the results page |
| Hardware accelerators | Credits for the cohort; one pack review per startup | One intro per cohort to a founder near production |
| Makerspaces, fablabs, design schools | "Build a product" weekend: idea → Factory Pack in a day (`PRD.md` §18, Phase 2) | A room and 15-30 participants |
| Crowdfunding platforms | Free packs for campaigns in pre-launch, offered through the platform's creator resources | Listing as a creator resource |
Makers and hobbyists are anti-ICP as buyers (`context/icp.md`) but welcome as free users: they are the funnel and the data.

## Rule 3: set a cap per event
Credits per team and a total per event, set before the event from the per-run cost above. Use the showcase gallery (opens at $0, `PRODUIT.md`) for demos; live runs only for participants.

## Measure (per event, in `outputs/`)
- Projects started, first version reached (wow), packs exported, LLM spend.
- **Referrals:** new projects from an invite link or "how did you hear about us"; intros given. This is our version of Verna's referral score; watch it more than sign-ups.
- Pull-through: packs from the event that later reach the tier 2 funnel (`context/metrics.md`, stages 7-10).

## Do not
- Present the demo factories as real to participants (`context/profile.md`).
- Collect founders' files beyond what the Studio needs; no founder data in this repo.
- Run a free event without a named owner for the follow-ups.
