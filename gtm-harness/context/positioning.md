# Positioning

**Hypothesis: to be validated by discovery.**

## The promise
**A committed landed price and a quality guarantee.**
- The founder gets one price per unit delivered to their warehouse, and we are held to it. That price covers:
  - factory price (FOB), freight, insurance and duties;
  - broker and port fees;
  - 3PL, QC and mold amortisation.

  This is the landed cost formula in `02_RECHERCHE/parallel/D_unit_economics.md`.
- If a lot fails inspection, we absorb the cost, not the founder.

**Why it matters:**
- The price in a crowdfunding campaign is fixed at pledge time, so any cost drift eats the margin. KONKR's RAM price "nearly tripled" ([GSMGoTech](https://www.gsmgotech.com/2026/03/ayaneo-konkr-pocket-fit-elite.html)).
- US duty now depends on the HTS line. Section 301 remains and the de minimis exemption is suspended ([CBP](https://content.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDHSCBP/bulletins/40b11c9), [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/24/2026-12669/indefinite-suspension-of-the-de-minimis-exemption-for-mail-shipments-and-new-postal-informal-entry)).

**How it links to thesis v2:** we can only commit to a price and to a quality level if we do the translation work ourselves:
- DFM;
- component choice;
- certification;
- the spec written in the factory's language.

## What no competitor publishes
Competitors count users, suppliers or brands. None publishes:
- its on-time delivery rate;
- its defect rate;
- its rejected lots.

Source: `02_RECHERCHE/parallel/B_concurrence.md`, "Lecture" §2.

- No Logo adds 25% to cost, and molds and specs remain the client's responsibility ([nologo.com/pricing](https://nologo.com/pricing); `02_RECHERCHE/parallel/00_SYNTHESE.md`, fact 6).
- Accio "does not carry the risk" (`02_RECHERCHE/parallel/B_concurrence.md` §2).

**Our proof asset:** publish our on-time rate and our defect rate from the first run onwards.

## What we say
- "One landed price, committed."
- "If the lot fails QC, it's on us."
- "We translate your product for the factory: DFM, components, certification."

## What we do not say
| Don't say | Why |
|---|---|
| "Cheaper" | Sourcing is already priced low: Accio costs $19.90 to $199 a month and agents charge 5 to 10% (`CLAUDE.md`). We cannot win on price, and a guarantee costs money. |
| "Faster" | Most documented delays are engineering, component or certification problems (`CLAUDE.md`, evidence 1). We cannot promise speed on those before we have delivery data. |
| "AI designs your product" | Text-to-CAD and "speak it into existence" are already on the market: RapidDirect, Adam, Zoo (`02_RECHERCHE/parallel/B_concurrence.md` §3). Design is not where launches slip. |
