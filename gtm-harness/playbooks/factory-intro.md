# Playbook: factory introduction

**Trigger:** a real founder's Factory Pack is ready to be quoted (W3-W4, deck slide 9), or a partner offers an intro. **Rule: reach factories through intermediaries, never cold.** Sources: deck slide 9 (W4); `04_LIVRABLE/GTM_Lessons_PhysicalLovableX.md`, synthesis §4 and the Crossbeam and BoomPop fiches (ecosystem partners and in-person trust beat cold outreach on this side). Everything below is Hypothesis until the first factory conversation.

## Step 1: pick 3-5 intermediaries (not factories)
- Types: sourcing agents, QC inspectors, 3PLs, CAD freelancers, hardware engineering studios (`context/personas/ecosystem-partner.md`).
- Score each on the 4Cs (Capacity, Capability, Commitment, Customers) before asking anything (GTM Strategist, "Tactical Partnership Guide 101", `04_LIVRABLE/GTM_Lessons_Substack_MajaVoje.md`).
- Hexa network intros are asked for, not assumed (deck slide 9, owners).

## Step 2: give first
Offer the intermediary something useful before the ask: the anonymised synthesis of why launches slip (from coded calls), or a sample Factory Pack for one of their own clients.

## Step 3: one precise ask
"Could you introduce me to one factory that would quote this real Factory Pack?" One pack, one factory, one quote. No capacity-sharing ask yet (assumption A4 comes later, `PRD.md` §3.1).

## Step 4: before the factory sees anything
- The founder agrees in writing that their pack is shared with this factory.
- An NDA is signed with the factory (deck slide 12; `PRD.md` §15).
- Remove any fictional demo data from what is shown; never present the demo factories, volumes or quotes as real (`context/profile.md`).
- Pack in EN + CN (`PRODUIT.md`).

## Step 5: measure
Log per factory (by type and region, not by contact name, until the relationship is formal): date the RFQ was sent, date of the quote, number of clarification rounds, whether the quote came back without back-and-forth. Target: a quote within 5 working days (Hypothesis, deck slide 12).

## Step 6: decide
- Factory refuses to share capacity → RFQ-only flow, infer capacity from quotes (`PRD.md` §3.1, A4).
- Quote returned without back-and-forth → count it as the factory-side PMF signal and add it to the documented case (W5-W6).
- Update `context/personas/factory-owner.md` objections after every conversation.

## Do not
- Blast RFQs to Alibaba or 1688 suppliers.
- Promise volumes, exclusivity or payment terms.
- Store factory contact details in this repo.
