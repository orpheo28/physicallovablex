# Playbook: build in public

**Trigger:** Hexa's go to go public. **Post-go only:** until then, nothing public names the project (`03_DISCOVERY/messages.md`). Cadences below are Hypothesis (our choice) unless sourced.

**Sources:** Elena Verna, Lovable (`02_RECHERCHE/lovable.md`, Vidéo 1): "growth is a trust problem"; employees encouraged to "build in public and grow their social followings and really reach your customer through your own team's voices"; "committed to launching every day" plus "tier one launches" every one to two months; the team "beeswarm[s]" on each other's posts. Anton Osika: GPT Engineer launched as an open-source tool with a demo video, then a waitlist, then Lovable (same file, Vidéo 4; deck slide 5). Maja Voje, ["Turn Building in Public into your most profitable GTM channel"](https://knowledge.gtmstrategist.com/p/turn-building-in-public-into-your): 30 minutes a day, and "Building in Public ≠ Bragging in Public" (`04_LIVRABLE/GTM_Lessons_Substack_MajaVoje.md`).

## Who posts
- **Founder-led first:** the founder's own account, 30 minutes a day, on what was learned: a coded delay cause, a pack finding, a factory round-trip. Real numbers with their labels, no vanity metrics (Voje).
- **Employee-led next:** everyone who ships posts what they shipped, in their own words. Marketing (when it exists) only amplifies tier-1 launches (Verna).

## Cadence
| Rhythm | What | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Each shipped release | Ship-and-post: one short post or changelog line per real change | "Refines now re-measure DFM on the CAD: 4-7 s" (`PRODUIT.md`) |
| Weekly | One learning from the field, anonymised | A delay cause from `03_DISCOVERY/verbatims.md`, with the founder's permission if recognisable |
| Every 1-2 months | **Tier-1 launch**: a bundle with a story and a demo video | Open Factory Pack format + production MCP spec, Osika-style open launch (`gtm/launch_video_script.md`) |
| From run 1 | **Trust brand:** publish on-time rate, defect rate, rejected lots per run | No competitor publishes them (`02_RECHERCHE/parallel/B_concurrence.md`); `PRD.md` §6 |

## Bee-swarming
In the first hours after any team or founder post, the rest of the team comments with substance (a detail, a question, a counter-example), not "great post". One person goes down the list at the end of the day (Verna's routine). Lighthouse ambassadors may join (`playbooks/lighthouse-ambassadors.md`), never scripted.

## Trust rules (growth is a trust problem)
- Every number carries its label: Measured, Sourced, Estimate, Fictional — demo data (`PRODUIT.md`).
- Never show a demo factory, quote or volume as real (`context/profile.md`).
- Publish the bad runs too: a late lot or a failed QC is part of the rate.
- Never name a late founder or their product without written permission; talk about the problem, never the backers' anger (`skills/signal-to-sequence`).

## Measure (monthly, in `skills/weekly-update`)
Posts shipped vs planned; inbound conversations that cite a post; intros from ambassadors. Followers alone do not count (`context/metrics.md`).
