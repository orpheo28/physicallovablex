## Product (two pieces)
- **The Studio:** describe a product in one sentence, see it in 3D with unit cost and a factory shortlist, refine by prompting, then "Make it" runs 13 stages to a Launch Dossier. Measured: first version in 18-24 s; refines 4-7 s (colour, feature) and 11-24 s (geometry) (`PRODUIT.md`).
- **The Factory Pack (the primitive):** a spec package a factory can quote without back-and-forth: structured spec, CAD, BOM with component risk, DFM alerts, certification checklist, estimated landed cost, questions for the factory in EN + CN, assumption register (`PRODUIT.md`).
- **Two entry modes:** "I have an idea" (funnel, data) and "I have a prototype" (GTM wedge: funded founders stuck before production) (`PRODUIT.md`).
