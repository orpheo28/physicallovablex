# Persona: the ecosystem partner (intermediary)

Last updated: 2026-09-27. **Hypothesis:** no partner has been approached. This persona exists because the plan reaches factories through intermediaries, not cold (deck slide 9, W4), and because partners can bring founders.

## Who (examples, Hypothesis on fit)
| Type | Why they matter | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware engineering / product-design studio | Sees founders at prototype stage, before the factory | Hypothesis |
| Crowdfunding or fulfilment service (e.g. the backer tools founders already run: PledgeBox, BackerKit) | Sees the campaign and the ship date | `outputs/accounts.csv` (AERIONN, Peak Design rows) |
| Sourcing agent, QC inspector, 3PL, CAD freelancer | Already holds factory trust | Deck slide 9, W4; `04_LIVRABLE/GTM_Lessons_PhysicalLovableX.md` (Crossbeam fiche) |

## Decision role
Refers founders or factories; does not buy. Can also be a blocker: a sourcing agent paid 5-10% ([Dragon Sourcing](https://www.dragonsourcing.com/top-10-cost-insights-for-hiring-a-china-sourcing-agent/)) may see a committed-price model as a threat (Hypothesis).

## How to choose them
Score each candidate on the 4Cs before any ask: Capacity, Capability, Commitment, Customers (access to the same founder). Source: GTM Strategist, "Tactical Partnership Guide 101" (`04_LIVRABLE/GTM_Lessons_Substack_MajaVoje.md`). Start with 3-5 candidates, not a programme.

## What they care about (Hypothesis)
- More good clients, less rework on their side.
- Not losing the client to us.
